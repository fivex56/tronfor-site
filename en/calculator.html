Why is staking not in the calculation?

Staking is frozen capital, not a cost, so it does not compare directly with a one-off payment. Where it does start to win is on burning, staking or renting.

It says “price in the bot”. Why?

Our feed is momentarily unavailable — updating, most likely. The burn figures stay correct, and the bot quotes the rental price for right now.
